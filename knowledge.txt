BOOKSTAKEN — KNOWLEDGE BASE

ABOUT BOOKSTAKEN

Bookstaken helps children develop a love for reading, curiosity, confidence, independent thinking, and imagination through books.

Bookstaken offers live 1-on-1 online sessions with college mentors. Mentors are positioned as supportive elder-sibling-like role models rather than traditional teachers or tutors.

Sessions focus on conversations around stories and books. Children can discuss characters, explore ideas, ask questions, express their opinions, and think out loud.

The goal is to help children become deep readers and independent thinkers while making reading enjoyable.

MENTORS / BOOKBUDDIES

A Bookbuddy is a mentor who reads, discusses, and explores books with students.

Bookbuddies are college students who guide children through stories, ideas, and conversations.

Bookbuddies are not traditional tutors. They aim to build curiosity, confidence, imagination, and intellectual growth.

Mentors may:
- Discuss chapters from novels.
- Explore characters and their decisions.
- Ask questions that encourage curiosity.
- Help children express their ideas confidently.
- Encourage imagination.
- Make reading exciting and enjoyable.

Selected mentors receive guidance in:
- Curiosity-led discussions.
- Encouraging expression.
- Asking thoughtful questions.
- Building reading excitement.
- Nurturing imagination.

BOOKSTAKEN EXPERIENCE

Bookstaken sessions are designed to feel like thoughtful conversations about books rather than classroom lectures.

The mentor and child explore stories together, discuss ideas, and encourage the child to express their own thoughts.

The aim is not simply to teach children how to read. The aim is to help them enjoy reading and develop curiosity, confidence, imagination, and independent thinking.
